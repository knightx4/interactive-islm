Supply shocks and stagflation

In late 1973 the price of crude oil roughly quadrupled in a few months. Within a year US inflation was above 12% and unemployment was on its way to 9%. Prices and joblessness rose together, which the standard story of the time said should not happen. This lesson shows why it does.
